Fleet booking, step 2 of 2 (A11-244): the scheduled fleet work takes leases from the lease ledger too, so it never lands in the middle of a capture. The hourly drift check (`fleet-drift.ts --record`) takes a shared `read` lease on every box no `maintain` lease holds; it reads beside a capture, because it sends only `GET /health` and `GET /progress`, the reads that already run during every capture. A box under `maintain` is not asked that hour: it is recorded in `skipped` as unread with its holder, and its last differences are carried. The daily freeze (`a11y-fleet-freeze-daily.service`) now runs `src/fleet-leases/freeze-daily.ts`, which takes an exclusive `maintain` lease per box, runs `freeze.yml --limit <the free boxes>` as batches, waits for the rest, and stops after 6 hours, naming any box it never got in `leased` in `runs/fleet-freeze-last-run.json` (carried into the hourly record's `freeze`) and failing the unit. The idle power-off produces its `lease-pending` (per box: a live `capture` or `maintain` lease, or a queued job that named it) and `batch-queued` (a job waiting for "any N" or `ready` boxes) signals from the ledger, and takes a non-queueing `maintain` lease across each `sleep.yml` dispatch. Re-run `pnpm run fleet:freeze-schedule` after merge to install the freeze unit's new `ExecStart`.
