Fleet booking, step 1 of 2 (A11-244): every fleet job leases its boxes through one lease ledger on the control plane, so jobs queue and never collide. The ledger is `/var/lib/a11y-fleet-leases/ledger.json` (`src/fleet-leases/`: a versioned JSON file written under a pid-checked mutex, with the booking rules, the estimated-start walk and the file kept apart). `capture` and `maintain` leases are exclusive; `read` is shared with `capture`; a ledger that cannot be read, or is of a version this code does not know, reads as every box held. The job service's in-memory claim is gone: a job joins the queue at its submission time (so a restart keeps every job's place), starts only when it holds the turn for boxes it can have (jobs are served in order; a job that names boxes reserves only those; a job for any N boxes or `ready` holds the head of the line so it is never starved), releases each box as soon as its own share of the pages is done, releases the lease when it ends (done, failed or cancelled), renews it every 30 s, and loses it 5 minutes after the last renewal if its service dies. `fleet job status` shows a queued job's `place: n of m`, its `estimated start` (or `unknown (why)`) and who it waits on, in text and in `--json` (`queue`); `fleet lease list [--json]` shows the leases and the queue. A box that leaves a running job, by three failed captures in a row or by a current difference in the hourly drift record (`runs/fleet-drift.json`), fails only its own unsent pages (`<box> left the fleet: <why>`, no longer moved to the other boxes), is named in `dropped`, and has its lease freed once its capture in flight ends; a box the drift record lists is not offered to a new job, and a job that names one fails at start. `a11y-fleet-jobs.service` gains the `a11y-fleet-leases` state directory. Step 2 adds the scheduled work (the drift check, the daily freeze, auto-off).
