The fleet job service takes jobs of cases, for lab's captures (A11-263, step 1 of 4). `fleet capture submit --cases <file>` submits a job whose every capture has its own id, URL and `/capture` body, sent exactly (the page job's defaults are not merged in), and an optional `group`: every case of a group is captured on one box, one after another and in file order, and a group is never split, even after a restart (a group moves only whole and only while untouched). A group may carry `skipWhen`, which skips it, with nothing sent and no record written, when the box's `/health` environment matches the named fields. `fleet job recapture <id> --cases a,b --label <key>` prints a new job ID for named cases of a `done` case job; each case is pinned to the box its group was captured on, the job asks for those boxes by name so it queues and leases like any named job (A11-244), and its records are `<case>.a<attempt>.json`. A job of cases or a re-capture is store version 2 (the service reads 1 and 2; a page job is still version 1, and an older service fails a case job by name). The manifest of a case job gains `cases`, `recaptureOf`, each capture's `case`, `group` and `attempt`, and each box's whole `environment`. A job of pages behaves as before. Revert: remove `--cases` and `recapture` from `cli.ts` and `remote.ts`; a page job reads and runs as it did.
