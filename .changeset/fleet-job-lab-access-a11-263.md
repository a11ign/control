The lab reaches the fleet job service, and the lab catalogue hands its capture jobs a label and boxes (A11-263, step 2 of 4). `ansible/fleet-jobs-lab-access.yml` makes `/root/.ssh/a11y-fleet-jobs` on the lab (never copied off it), puts its public half in the control plane's `authorized_keys` as `restrict,command="… src/fleet-jobs/gate.ts"`, checks control out on the lab at `/opt/a11y-fleet-cli`, writes `/etc/a11ign/fleet.env` and `/usr/local/bin/fleet`, and reads back from the lab that `fleet lease list --json` answers and that `id` over the key is refused. `src/fleet-jobs/gate.ts` runs `remote.ts` only in the form `controlPlaneTransport` sends (single-quoted arguments, no shell) and refuses every other command with exit 2; ADR 0004 gives the reasons. In `lab-job.yml`, `label` is a new published parameter, REQUIRED by every job that captures (`capture`, `capture-only`, both `capture-acceptance`, `capture-real-pages`, `retrain`, `everything`, `evidence-check`, `stability`, `gate-stability`, `gate-probe-order`, `capture-check`): a launch without it is refused naming the parameter. Those jobs also set `A11Y_FLEET_BOXES` (the `workers` or `worker` name or count, else `ready`), `A11Y_FLEET_LABEL` and `A11Y_FLEET_WAIT` (the job's own timeout); a lab older than step 3 never reads them. `lab:pipeline` takes `--label=<ticket key>` and passes it to the stages that take one (a pipeline that captures is refused without it), and a `--qualify-sha` run that names no label is booked as `qualify-<row>`. Revert: remove the three variables and the `label` parameter from the catalogue, the `--label` flag from `lab-pipeline.ts`, the default label from `qualification-run.ts`, and `gate.ts` with its play; remove the `a11y-lab-fleet-jobs` line from root's `authorized_keys` on the control plane.
